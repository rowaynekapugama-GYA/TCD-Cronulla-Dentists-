# Updating your website: The Cronulla Dentists dashboard

Your website has a dashboard where the team can change wording, swap photos, update hours and details,
write articles and see enquiries. This guide walks through each task step by step.

**Where:** https://www.thecronulladentists.com.au/admin
**Login:** your email address and the password GYA sent you. If you forget it, ask GYA to set a new one.

## Before you write anything: the advertising rules (AHPRA)

Dental practices must follow AHPRA's advertising rules for anything published on the website, including
the dashboard's blog and page copy. In short:

- No testimonials or reviews about clinical care (patient stories, star ratings, quotes about treatment).
- No claims such as "pain-free", "painless", "guaranteed", "best", "expert", "leading" or "specialist"
  (unless registered as a specialist).
- Any offer must state the exact price, what is included and the offer period.
- No pressure wording ("book now before it is too late").

Stick to plain, factual information. If you are unsure, ask GYA before publishing.

## The one thing to remember: Save, then Publish website

Saving a page stores your change in the dashboard. It does not change the website yet. When you are
happy with everything, go to the **Dashboard** home page and click **Publish website**. The website is
rebuilt with all your changes and is live about two to three minutes later.

The yellow notice at the top of the dashboard tells you when there are changes waiting to be published.
Enquiries never need publishing.

## Finding your way around

The menu on the left:

- **Dashboard**: the Publish website button, quick actions and the latest enquiries.
- **Pages**: every page on the website.
- **Blog Posts**: articles for the Dental advice and news section.
- **Media Library**: photos.
- **Team**: the dentists on the About page.
- **Enquiries**: messages from the website forms.
- **SEO**: how each page appears in Google.
- **Site Settings**: phone, hours, address, booking link, health funds and feature switches.

The top bar has **View site** to open the live website in a new tab. **Sign out** is at the bottom of the menu.

## Task 1: change the wording on a page

1. Click **Pages** and open the page. The page itself appears on the left, exactly as visitors see it,
   with a panel on the right.
2. **Click the words you want to change and type.** A blue outline shows what you are editing. Press
   Enter (or click somewhere else) when you are done with that piece.
3. Some headings are styled by the design (all capitals, or one word in a different colour). Clicking one
   of those opens it in the panel on the right instead: change it there and the page updates.
4. Click **Save changes** at the top right. Then click **Publish website** (top bar, or on the Dashboard)
   when you want it live.

Other things in the editor:

- **Desktop / Mobile** at the top shows how the page looks on a computer or a phone.
- The **Content** tab lists everything on the page, section by section, if you prefer to edit there.
- The **Sections** tab shows the page from top to bottom. Click a section to jump to it. On service pages
  and information pages you can drag the lower sections into a new order, add one or remove one.
- **Links do nothing while you edit**, so you cannot click away by accident. Use **View** to open the
  live page.

Shortcuts that work in the panel's text boxes:

- `**words**` makes the words bold
- `{{phone}}` and `{{email}}` insert the practice phone number and email (they stay correct if the details
  change in Site Settings)
- `[link text](/services/)` makes a link to another page on the site

Saved changes show on the live website after Publish website.

## Task 2: swap a photo

1. Open the page and **click the photo**. "Click to change photo" appears when you hover over one.
2. In the panel, click **Choose from library / Upload**. Pick a photo from the Media library tab, or use the
   Upload tab to choose one from your computer and describe it (alt text).
3. Check the **Alt text**: a few words saying what is in the photo.
4. Save changes, then Publish website.

**Use the built-in photo** puts back the photo the site was designed with.

To replace one photo everywhere it is used: open **Media Library**, click the photo, then **Choose a new
file** under Replace this photo. Every page using it updates at the next Publish website. Keep a similar
shape (landscape or portrait) so the layout stays the same.

## Task 3: write a blog article

1. **Blog Posts** > **Write a new article**.
2. Type the title and a one or two sentence summary.
3. Write the article in the editor. The toolbar has headings, bold and italic, bullet and numbered
   lists, quotes, links and **Photo** to add a picture from the Media Library.
4. On the right: choose a featured image, tick a category (or add a new one), check the author and date.
5. Click **Publish article**. To keep working on it without publishing, click **Save draft** instead.
6. Publish website from the Dashboard. The first published article makes the Dental advice and news
   section appear on the website, with a link in the footer.

To take an article off the site, open **Blog Posts** and click **Unpublish**, then Publish website.
**Delete** removes it for good.

## Task 4: update opening hours or practice details

1. **Site Settings** > **Opening hours** tab.
2. Each day has a Closed tick box, an opens time, a closes time (24-hour, for example 17:00) and the
   wording shown on the site (filled in for you, for example 8:00am to 5:00pm).
3. Click **Save settings**, then Publish website. The hours update on every page, in the footer and in
   Google's listing of the practice.

The **Practice** tab has the phone number, email and address. **Booking and payment** has the online booking
link, the health fund lines and the **Book online pop-up**: when someone clicks Book online they are asked to
choose The Cronulla Dentists or The Caringbah Dentists. You can change each practice's name, address line,
booking link and short note, drag them into a different order, or untick "Ask which location" to send
Book online straight to the Cronulla booking page. **Features** has the switches for the kids no-gap offer, emergency
dentistry and Zip/Afterpay: turning one on or off shows or hides everything about it across the site, so
check with GYA first.

## Task 5: change how a page appears in Google (SEO)

1. Click **SEO** in the menu to see every page's meta title and description with a Good / Short / Too long
   flag, or open a page and click its **SEO** tab.
2. Edit the **Meta title** (under 60 characters) and **Meta description** (70 to 155 characters). The
   preview above the boxes shows roughly how the page will look in Google.
3. Optionally choose a **Share image** for Facebook and messages.
4. Save changes, then Publish website.

Leave "Hide this page from search engines" unticked unless GYA says otherwise.

## Task 6: the team

**Team** lists the dentists shown on the About page. Drag the handle to reorder them, click **Hide** to
take someone off the website without deleting them, or **Edit** to change the photo or bio. Names, roles
and qualifications must match the practice's records and the AHPRA register; do not add or change
qualifications without checking.

## Task 7: enquiries

Every message sent through the website's contact form is emailed to reception and also listed under
**Enquiries**, with the date, the page it came from and the details. Open one to add notes and tick
**Followed up**. **Export to CSV** downloads the list for Excel.

## If something looks wrong

Nothing you do in the dashboard can break the website's design. If a page looks odd after publishing, the
usual cause is an empty box or a stray `**`. Open the page, fix it, save and publish again. GYA can also
restore an earlier version of any page.
